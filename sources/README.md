The final article was downloaded on 2026-09-06 from the public PDF linked on
[Siwan Anderson’s publications page](https://sites.google.com/view/siwan-anderson/home/published-papers):

- `paper.pdf`: [author-posted final article](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1GGvPbS3WOeyfbWfTIU2SI3_4DKJqL8Op).
- `paper.txt`: `pdftotext -layout paper.pdf paper.txt`; input to the numerical table comparison.
- `working-paper-2005.pdf` and `.txt`: [University of Houston working paper](https://www.uh.edu/academics/sos/econ/documents/caste.pdf). It is not the 2009 unpublished paper cited in the final article.
- `previous-stata-output.txt`: [public Stata 17 log](https://osf.io/download/32jd4/) from the [2022 OSF reproduction](https://osf.io/h2u96/). It reproduces Table 3(1).
- `osf-files.json`: [public OSF file metadata](https://api.osf.io/v2/nodes/h2u96/files/osfstorage/). The archived household file has the same SHA-256 as the supplied file.
- `published_regression.csv`: numeric transcription of every coefficient and SE displayed in Tables 3–4 and Table 5’s final column, generated by `src/compare_published.R`.

Article PDFs and extracted article text are local caches excluded from git. `make sources`
downloads the final PDF and regenerates its text when absent. It needs `curl` and Poppler’s
`pdftotext` (`brew install poppler` on macOS). No data download is needed: the small original
replication files are included under `data/original/` with their original license.

The supplemental 2005 PDF is not an input to the calculations. Neither that draft nor the
2022 reproduction is used as a substitute for the final published tables.
