These are research opportunities arising from the review, not completed new studies. Novelty has not been established through a new literature search. The current replication uses already-seen outcomes; further analyses of this sample are exploratory unless a genuinely independent confirmation is reserved.

The most informative new-data project is the actual-supplier study. Its first deliverable should be a data and design memo using `build-data` and `design-analysis`: define feasible supply, specify the target comparison, record what has already been learned, and identify a source of variation whose assumptions can be defended. A manuscript can then use `write-empirical-paper` to keep the claim, generated evidence, figures, and prose synchronized.
